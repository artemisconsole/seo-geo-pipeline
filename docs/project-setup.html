Goal · KPI · Observe

Goal is shared; KPI has two levels; Observe metrics are the leading signals we watch.

Goal

Same for every client — the business outcome.

  • Lead — direct booking, revenue
  • Lead pipeline — book-now clicks, contact-us, enquiries
Every tier is driving the same goal. Only the KPI bar and the effort to clear it differ.

KPI

Two levels. Higher tiers must clear the higher bar.

Level 1Growth baseline

Every tracked metric trends up; Organic + AI traffic targets met.

Level 2+ Visibility

Plus keyword ranking & AI Visibility targets met.

Observe

Leading signals we watch between KPI checkpoints.

  • Branded clicks
  • Total keyword ranking improvement
  • GBP impressions & interactions
  • GSC impressions
  • Direct traffic
  • Revenue-page traffic

Brand / website strength

A new brand on a new site needs more effort, and ranking + KPI arrive on a longer timeline — so the quick wins differ too.

New & weakmost effort

Low authority, thin index, little brand search. Rankings take longer; KPI timeline is longer.

  • Quick wins: indexation, core money pages, GBP setup, brand-SERP ownership
  • Heavier content velocity + technical foundation
  • Expect slower ranking; lead first on traffic + branded signals

Growingmedium

Some authority and index coverage; a few pages ranking.

  • Quick wins: striking-distance keywords, on-page, content-gap fills
  • Balance new content with optimising what exists
  • Ranking + AI visibility start to move within the quarter

Established & strongleast effort

High authority, broad index, strong brand search.

  • Quick wins: cannibalisation, CTR rewrites, CRO, defend & expand
  • Lean into topical coverage + GEO citation
  • Faster ranking; push straight for Level 2 KPI
