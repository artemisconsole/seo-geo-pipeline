NEW · PIVOTAL ★ P04f · Quick Win & Win Strategy The plan says what to do; this decides what to do first for fast results, and the strategy to win — hit KPI and drive bookings, submits, purchases, revenue.

A · DO FIRST Quick wins (high impact, low effort)

  • Keyword cannibalisation — merge/route competing URLs, pick the money page
  • Striking distance — positions 5–20 with intent, push into the top 3
  • Money-page on-page — fix title/H1/meta/schema/CTA on service & booking pages
  • Technical quick-fix — index blockers, speed, missing schema on revenue pages
  • GBP / local — complete profile, posts, fix NAP
  • CTR rewrite — top-10 pages with weak CTR → sharper title/meta

B · WIN Winning strategy (path to KPI + revenue)

  • Topical coverage — own the whole problem-pattern cluster
  • Template / programmatic — best-time-to-visit, food guide, best-hotel-near-X at scale
  • Local SEO — GBP, local landing pages, reviews, map pack
  • Backlinks / authority — digital PR, lift GEO citations
  • GEO coverage — answer chunks, entities, proof so AI search cites you
  • CRO — turn traffic into booking / submit / purchase

Each pillar carries an experiment: hypothesis → test → win/lose metric → deadline.
